Grammar Focus • Past of be
12–14 min

Use was / were to talk about what was true in the past.

A. Grammar map

AFFIRMATIVE

SubjectPast of beExample
IwasI was born in Brazil.
YouwereYou were very young.
He / ShewasShe was seven.
WewereWe were at school.
TheywereThey were in Toronto.

NEGATIVE

FormExample
was not → wasn’tShe wasn’t in college. • I wasn’t born in Italy.
were not → weren’tThey weren’t in London. • We weren’t at school.

QUESTIONS + SHORT ANSWERS

QuestionShort answer
Were you in class yesterday?Yes, I was. / No, I wasn’t.
Was your first teacher friendly?Yes, she was. / No, she wasn’t.
Were your parents born in Brazil?Yes, they were. / No, they weren’t.
Notice: I / he / she / it → WAS   •   you / we / they → WERE
Born chunk: I was born in Brazil. • My parents were born in Italy.
