Family connections

Listen to a family interview and use possessive adjectives, ’s and s’ to say who is connected to whom.

Hear key family details.
Choose my / your / his / her / our / their.
Use names + ’s and plural s’.
Explain connections respectfully.

3

Language lab • Possessive adjectives

Put the possessive adjective before the noun.

10 min
SubjectPossessive adjectiveFamily example
ImyMy brother is Leo.
youyourIs Rosa your mother?
hehisHis wife is Elena.
sheherHer husband is Marco.
weourOur parents are Elena and Marco.
theytheirTheir mother is Rosa.
Important: use her mother, not she mother. Use their children, not they children.

Change the point of view

4

Names + ’s / plural s’

Show who is connected to whom.

12 min
FormExample
one person / name + ’sElena’s brother is Daniel.
plural ending in s + ’The parents’ children are Sofia and Leo.
irregular plural + ’sThe children’s grandmother is Rosa.
two names + ’s after the last nameElena and Marco’s children are Sofia and Leo.
Notice: possessive adjectives do not take an apostrophe: his brother, her mother, their children.

Family connection challenge

Speak • Who is connected to whom?

Use the picture or create a safe fictional example.

8–10 min
Model:
A: Whose mother is Rosa?
B: She is Elena and Daniel’s mother.
A: Is Daniel Sofia’s father?
B: No, he isn’t. He is her uncle.
