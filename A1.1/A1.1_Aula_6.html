8–9 min

JulyAugustSeptemberOctoberNovemberDecember
Compound ordinal rule
Only the last part becomes ordinal: 21st = twenty-first; 22nd = twenty-second; 23rd = twenty-third; 24th = twenty-fourth; 31st = thirty-first. 20th alone = twentieth.
