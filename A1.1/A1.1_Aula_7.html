Contact Repair Challenge

Find the problem. Repair it clearly.

7–8 min

A. Find the problem
1. My number is 305-555-2803. Is that 305-555-2308? No, it’s .
2. My email is ana_lima@study.org. Ana dot Lima? No, ana lima.
3. Garcia? G-A-R-S-I-A? No. It’s .

B. Choose the best repair phrase
1. You cannot hear the number: (A say again / B Is that 2308? / C spell that)
2. You need a surname spelling: (A Is that 2308? / B spell that / C say again)
3. You want to check 2308: (A spell / B say again / C Is that 2308?)

C. Pair challenge
Student A reads one detail. Student B asks for repetition or spelling, confirms and writes it.
