Plan to or be going to?

The meanings overlap; notice the speaker's focus.

11 min

plan to + base verb

Foreground the intention or strategy.

I plan to compare three courses.

am / is / are going to + base verb

Present a future action as decided, often with time or detail.

I'm going to email a school tomorrow.

1. Name the strategy: I ___ three universities before I apply.
2. Decided action + time: I ___ the course next Monday.
3. Name her intention: She ___ for two scholarships.
4. Decided action: We ___ a careers adviser after lunch.
5. Name their long-term plan: They ___ their own studio.
6. Decided negative action: He ___ until he checks the cost.
