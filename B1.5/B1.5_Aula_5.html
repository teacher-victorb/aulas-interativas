4

Language Lab: future ability

Describe what will or will not be possible in the future.

10 min

WILL BE ABLE TO

Name a future ability or possibility.

We will be able to start soon.

WILL NOT BE ABLE TO

Name a future limit or inability.

We will not be able to finish without help.

!
Complete form
will + be + able + to + base verb. Do not say will can or omit be/to. Question: Will you be able to attend?
1. With more time, we ___ the course.
2. Without internet, she ___ the meeting.
3. If I save enough, I ___ for the training.
4. They ___ the deadline unless they get support.
5. After the course, he ___ the software.
6. ___ attend if the schedule changes?
