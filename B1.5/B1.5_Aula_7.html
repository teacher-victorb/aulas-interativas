A strong vision
It is specific enough to feel real and concise enough to remember.
1. In the future, I ___ build a balanced and meaningful life.
2. Professionally, I ___ work on projects that help people.
3. This matters ___ I value growth and connection.
4. First, I ___ complete a specialised course.
5. If I practise consistently, I ___ communicate with confidence.
6. Even when progress is slow, I want to ___.
