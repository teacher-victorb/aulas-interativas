Hope, dream or wish?

Choose the structure that matches your relationship with the future.

12 min

I wish I could...

A desire blocked by a present limit.

I wish I could travel more, but I don't have time.

I dream of...

An important, vivid or distant life aspiration.

I dream of opening a small café.

I hope to...

A wanted future that you see as possible.

I hope to visit Iceland next year.

+
Form
hope to visit | dream of visiting | wish I could visit

Choose the best complete form

1. I am waiting for my visa, but the trip is possible. I ___ Japan next year.
2. Writing a novel has been my ambition for years. I ___ one someday.
3. I want to learn the piano, but I have no free time now. I ___ it.
4. The final decision belongs to my company. I ___ the promotion.
5. Owning a creative studio is my big life ambition. I ___ my own studio.
6. My passport has expired, so I cannot travel now. I ___ abroad this month.
Choose a relationship and add your goal.
