Language Lab: connect your aspirations

Organise goals, reasons, steps and contrasts in one clear paragraph.

10 min

GOALS + REASONS

State an aspiration and explain its importance.

I hope to... because...

STEPS + BALANCE

Add a milestone and show another side.

First, I plan to... However, I also...

!
A clear paragraph
Move from general to specific: future focus → personal goal → professional goal → reason → first milestone → balance.
1. I hope to build a creative career ___ I enjoy solving problems.
2. I want more balance, ___ I plan to protect my weekends.
3. I would like to lead a team. ___, I do not want work to control my life.
4. ___, I plan to finish one professional course.
5. I hope to travel. I ___ want a stable home.
6. ___, I want both goals to support each other.
