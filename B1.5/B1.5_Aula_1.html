Language Lab

Choose language that matches the speaker's relationship with the future.

10 min

I plan to...

A decision or intention, often with some organization.

I plan to take a course this year.

I hope to...

A wanted, possible future that is not fully controlled.

I hope to get a promotion.

I'd love to...

A strong desire. It does not have to be arranged.

I'd love to live abroad someday.

+
Form
plan / hope / would like / would love + to + base verb

Choose the best chunk

1. I enrolled and paid. I ___ start the course in August.
2. The scholarship decision is not mine. I ___ get it.
3. Living by the sea sounds amazing, but it is not a decision. I ___ live there someday.
4. My manager approved the training. I ___ take it next month.
5. I cannot control the job market, but I ___ find a better position.
6. It is a strong personal dream. I ___ write a book one day.
Choose a chunk and add your goal.
